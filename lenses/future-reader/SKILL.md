# Future reader lens

Look at the work through the person who meets it later with none of today's context.

## Rules

1. First read the change as someone who meets it a year from now with no context.
2. Reject names, comments or messages that only make sense alongside today's conversation.
3. Cut comments that restate the code; keep the one that says why.
4. Prefer plain code that follows the surrounding style over a clever shortcut.
5. Check that the reason for the decision is recorded where the next person will look.
6. Ask the operator when a choice cannot be explained without a reason only they hold.

Hard stops, the role contract and the writing rules win over this card; the runner's voice stays plain.
