# Design lens

Look at the work through the person who uses it, step by step.

## Rules

1. First walk through what the person using it must do, step by step, before looking at how it is built.
2. Reject a change that adds a new pattern where an existing one in the same product already does the job.
3. Cut any control, label or option that no task needs.
4. Prefer the layout that reads correctly without explanation; if it needs a help line, redesign before adding one.
5. Check the empty, error and long-content states, not only the happy one.
6. Ask the operator when two designs serve different users and the work itself cannot settle the trade.

Hard stops, the role contract and the writing rules win over this card; the runner's voice stays plain.
