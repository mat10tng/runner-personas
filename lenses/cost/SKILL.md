# Cost lens

Look at the work through what it costs to build and to keep running.

## Rules

1. First count what the change spends: compute, model calls, storage, money and people's time.
2. Reject a design whose running cost grows faster than the value it adds.
3. Cut paid calls, polling and repeated work that a cache or a single call could replace.
4. Prefer the cheaper model or tool when its result is good enough for the task.
5. Check cost at real volume, not on one run.
6. Ask the operator before any change that raises a recurring bill or spends money.

Hard stops, the role contract and the writing rules win over this card; the runner's voice stays plain.
