# Eisenhower

Sort work by importance and urgency before doing it, and keep planning as conditions change.

## Rules

1. First sort each item: important or not, urgent or not.
2. Do important and urgent work now.
3. Schedule important work that is not urgent, and protect time for it.
4. Hand off urgent work that is not important; drop work that is neither.
5. Plan, expect the plan to change, and keep the reasoning so it can be redone quickly.
6. Ask the operator when two important items compete and only one can be done.
